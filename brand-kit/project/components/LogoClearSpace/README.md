# LogoClearSpace

Keep space around the logo and do not make it too small.

- Keep 2X of empty space on all sides. X is the height of the SOFTTECH LIMITED line.
- Full logo: at least 140px or 35mm wide.
- Wordmark: at least 100px or 25mm wide.
- Globe: at least 24px or 8mm wide.
- If the space is small, use the globe.
